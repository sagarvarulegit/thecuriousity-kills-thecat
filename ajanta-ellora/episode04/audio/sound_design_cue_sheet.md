# Episode 04 Sound Design Cue Sheet

**Mix intent:** narration stays intelligible and forward; the courtyard should feel open, with restrained music and sparse, naturalistic transitions. All tool and stone foley below is editorial reconstruction, not a claim about recorded historical sound.

| Act | In point | Ambience / score / foley | Direction |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Act I — The Gap | 00:00 | Open courtyard wind, distant birds, low restrained drone; a soft stone resonance on the first bridge reveal. | Keep the court spacious. Avoid battle drums or fabricated quarry spectacle. |
| Act II — A Temple in Sequence | 00:45 | Subtle low strings under a slow rock-to-void transition; quiet granular stone movement only as editorial foley. | The foley is a present-day reconstruction cue, not a claim about recorded eighth-century sounds. |
| Act III — The Route Through the Air | 01:39 | Light airy courtyard bed; gentle tonal marker as the route traces gateway to Nandi to hall. | Let the route and voice carry the sequence; no footsteps unless clearly stylized. |
| Act IV — How to Leave a Bridge | 02:26 | Muted chisel and scrape foley in short, separated gestures; let the ambience open as the void is revealed. | Label the whole sequence as reconstructed. No unsupported machinery, ropes, or tool-specific process. |
| Act V — Reading the Stone | 03:28 | Reduce music; use close, dry stone texture and a restrained low note at the bridge ends. | Avoid invented stress cracks, load calculations, or exaggerated groaning stone. |
| Act VI — The Missing Span | 04:24 | Thin the music and leave a clear pocket of courtyard air at the collapsed span; restrained falling-stone tail only if shown as an illustrative reconstruction. | Do not imply the time or cause of collapse. |
| Act VII — Architecture as Procession | 05:18 | Warm, measured strings with distant open-air ambience; slight lift as the sacred route resolves. | Keep devotional imagery respectful and avoid generic exoticized sound palettes. |
| Act VIII — The Mountain Reconnected | 06:13 | Return to the opening wind motif; let it resolve into a low, warm plinth teaser tone. | No abrupt stinger. Leave room for the next episode question. |

## Mix notes

- Keep narration at a consistent perceived loudness; duck music under every spoken line.
- Use a single restrained tonal motif across the episode so the bridge reveal, lost-span pause, and Episode 05 handoff feel connected.
- Keep foley brief and plausible. Do not add dramatic cracking, collapse, or tool sounds to imply undocumented events.
- Do not use faux-temple bells or regionally generic 'exotic' cues; the setting is an open stone courtyard and the story is carried by architecture.
