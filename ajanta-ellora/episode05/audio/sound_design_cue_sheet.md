## Mix notes

- Duck music beneath every spoken line and keep the opening courtyard ambience consistent with Episode 04.
- Avoid faux-temple bells, generic exoticized palettes, and dramatic stone stress or collapse effects.
- Treat all stone texture as editorial sound design, not historical field recording.
- Let the final motif bridge into the next episode’s focus on narrative sculpture.
