At Ellora’s Kailasa Temple, a procession of carved elephants runs beneath the raised plinth. They appear to carry the temple, but the relief figures are part of the rock-cut lower storey, not freestanding pillars. This episode looks at how sculpture gives architectural weight a vivid, living image—and separates what the stone shows from interpretations of what it may mean.
