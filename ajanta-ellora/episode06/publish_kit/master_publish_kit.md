## Description
Suspended thirty feet in the open air, monolithic stone bridges span the 100-foot volcanic chasm of Kailasa Temple (Cave 16) at Ellora. There is zero mortar, zero separate arches, and zero structural keystones. They were carved from the living volcanic basalt from the top down, hollowing out the void beneath before the courtyard floor even existed.

In this episode, we investigate the terrifying structural gamble behind Ellora's floating sky bridges, the top-down 7-day shikhara revelation of master builder Kokasa, the 17-meter freestanding victory pillars, the luminous white shell-lime plaster that once made the temple gleam like Mount Kailash, and the 1682 demolition siege by Emperor Aurangzeb that failed to bring the mountain down.
