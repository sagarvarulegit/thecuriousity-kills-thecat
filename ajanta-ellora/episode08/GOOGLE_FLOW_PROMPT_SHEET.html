🎬 Director's Production Directive: Reliving the Era Through Their Eyes

Every single video clip in this episode must put the audience directly inside the 5th-century mountain workshop. Viewers must feel the grit of the clay underfoot, hear the acoustic clinking of bronze and iron against volcanic basalt, watch the blinding sun beam reflected through cave darkness, and see the squirrel-hair brush lay down wet lapis lazuli on ivory plaster. Strictly zero magical glowing or fantasy tropes: 100% authentic human craftsmanship.
