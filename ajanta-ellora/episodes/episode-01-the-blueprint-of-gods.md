## The Smart Production Pyramid (API Budget Optimization)

**75 Micro-beats does NOT mean 75 separate AI image or video calls.**

* **Generate only 22 Master Hero Images (4K/8K)**: We use the **1-to-3 Multi-Cam Crop Technique** (Wide establishing $\rightarrow$ 200% punch-in close-up $\rightarrow$ pan/scan detail) to extract 50+ cuts from these master images.
* **Animate only 7 Key Action Moments**: Reserve video generation (Runway Gen-3 / Kling 1.5) strictly for physical/particle moments (sparks, steam, molten wax, explosive impact).
* **Motion Graphics & Archival (15+ cuts)**: Utilize 2D animated blueprints, statistical metric cards ("140 MPa"), and public domain satellite/archival visuals at $0 cost.
